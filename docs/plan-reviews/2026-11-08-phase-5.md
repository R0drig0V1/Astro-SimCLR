# Plan review — phase 5 (self-supervised pretraining) — 2026-11-08

## Summary

One round was run. It found 1 critical, 8 high, 4 medium and 1 low findings, all accepted.
The technical recipe (5.3) stands as it was; the study around it was rebuilt.

Main changes:
- a pre-registered H1;
- the shift question out of scope;
- stronger baselines: random init, SSL on the labelled images only, Zoobot;
- label budgets per class with a hierarchical bootstrap;
- the exclusion of every CD21 object from the corpus;
- the archive measured, about 20 TB, with an `ingest-archive` spec;
- a zero-download pilot (5.0b) that gates the download request;
- an HP policy;
- a compute budget, diagnostics and cuts.

A note from the integrator: the reviewer downloaded and then deleted a small archive sample
to measure sizes. The user had not yet approved downloads, and the user was told.
