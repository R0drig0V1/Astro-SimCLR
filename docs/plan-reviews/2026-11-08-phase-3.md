# Plan review — phase 3 (newer benchmarks and the raw-alert dataset) — 2026-11-08

## Summary

Round 1 ran on the first draft. Round 2 ran after task 3.0's research.

| Round | Critical | High | Medium | Low | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 6 | 4 | 1 | all accepted |
| 2 | 1 | 5 | 3 | 1 | all accepted; scope cut to R5 (5 classes, public sources) |

The integrator verified the decisive finding, F3, independently. Within 1.5″ of a Train
alert there are 24 of 500 Validation rows (Bogus 20 of 100) and 27 of 500 Test rows (Bogus
21 of 100). It reaches back into phase 2: the 2.5 decision splits are being regrouped
before the CV starts.
