# Plan review — phase 2 (replicate and improve the stamp classifier) — 2026-11-08

## Summary

Three adversarial rounds ran (the maximum), against the paper, ALeRCE's vendored TF1 code
(`alercebroker/pipeline@a3705ac`) and **the published M1 checkpoint and its event logs**,
which the adversary read without TensorFlow.

| Round | Critical | High | Medium | Low | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 4 | 6 | 2 | all accepted |
| 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 1 | all accepted |
| 3 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 1 | all accepted |

The integrator verified the decisive facts in the checkpoint:
- the feature BN's `moving_mean` is all 0 and its `moving_variance` all 1, while `gamma` and
  `beta` were trained;
- `beta2_power = 0.999^4782`, so β2 = 0.999 and the best checkpoint is from about
  iteration 4,780;
- the dropout branches switch on the never-fed `keras_learning_phase`.

The user changed the goal during the review (2026-11-07): replicate the paper, not the
2022 legacy numbers. The user delegated the scope decisions to the integrator: 2.5 starts
with the architecture, and the without-features gate is advisory.

Verdict: ready to dispatch 2.1, 2.2a and 2.3. The round-3 fixes were applied but not
re-attacked, because the round limit was reached.
